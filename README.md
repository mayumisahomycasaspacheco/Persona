# Persona
Persona is a simple console base contact management system made in c++  and objetive C.

![Persona](https://cdn.hackclub.com/01a0db71-8740-7f5e-9440-9cb5410a34e6/image.png)

## What does it do?
Persona lets you manage a small contact list directly from the console. You can add contacts with their name and number, and list all the contacts you've saved so far.

Everything is handled through a simple menu, so you can keep track of the people who matter most to you without losing anyone the way.

## How to use it?
1. Go to the releases section of this repository.
2. Download the .exe file.
3. Open the program.

Tha's it. Your contact list is ready to go.
